# UI interaction references

These sources inform VINote's React Native adaptations; no browser DOM, CSS runtime, gallery or CLI registry is bundled.

- Amicro design system: https://github.com/Subhan-code/Amicro--Micro-transitions- . Canvas/card separation, selection pills and cubic-bezier (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1); adapted to native Animated and reduced-motion preferences.
- Uiverse, 0x-Sarthak, hungry-penguin-30: https://uiverse.io/0x-Sarthak/hungry-penguin-30 . Active press scale 0.95 adapted to PrimaryButton. Studied through the installed uiverse-design curated Galaxy library, described by its Skill as MIT-licensed.
- Uiverse, JkHuger, sweet-starfish-100: https://uiverse.io/JkHuger/sweet-starfish-100 . Staggered bar scale rhythm adapted to LoadingDots, with finite cycles and AppState stop/resume. This is an operation indicator, not a recorded-audio waveform.

Uiverse Galaxy source and license: https://github.com/uiverse-io/galaxy . Original authors retain ownership of their designs. The project-specific adaptations preserve the source attribution here and in the development journal.
